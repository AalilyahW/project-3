# Project Retrospective & Final Reflection

### 1. What Went Well?
*   **Rigorous Scope Control:** Transitioning the project to a curated educational showcase allowed me to dig deeply into modern layout mechancoding, external asset integration, and downloaded typography configurations without missing deadline milestones.
*   **Deployment Pipeline Efficiency:** Setting up a predictable file architecture and verifying local adjustments before pushing directly to the main branch kept the pipeline simple and stress-free.

### 2. What Could Be Improved?
*   **Asset Management Lead Time:** Sourcing high-resolution editorial assets and tracking down their correct creator attribution names took longer than expected during the active coding window. Gathering a media log earlier would have optimized my initial layout momentum. Additionally, refining design transitions and allowing more patience for the iterative process—especially while bringing my vision to life on GitHub—will help improve future workflows 

### 3. What Will I Do Differently Next Time?
*   **Proactive Asset Inventories:** In future web design sprints, I will build a dedicated asset worksheet (containing image paths, source titles, and artist credits) during the Week 1 scoping phase. Planning this out beforehand will allow me to enter the active coding phase with a clear execution strategy rather than jumping straight in.
